How I Build a Project

  • 1. Discovery: goals, audience, constraints, channels.
  • 2. Direction: references, moodboard, script, storyboard.
  • 3. Production: styleframes, animation, sound, revisions.
  • 4. Delivery: platform versions, source files, handoff notes.
